## Design Context

### Users

Primary: College students quickly checking meal options.
Secondary: Mess managers updating the daily menus via the admin portal.
Context: Primarily mobile usage, quick interactions between classes or during meal times.

### Brand Personality

Useful, fun, and easy.
Young and full of personality without being edgy. Approachable, pragmatic, and delightful.

### Aesthetic Direction

- Visual Tone: Friendly, vibrant, and soft (e.g., rounded corners, clear typography, playful micro-interactions).
- Theme: Automatic light and dark mode adaptation based on system preferences.
- Layout: Mobile-first, fluid, and highly responsive. Avoid dense, intimidating corporate forms.

### Design Principles

1. **Frictionless Utility**: Core tasks (like signing up, logging in, or checking the menu) must be effortless and fast.
2. **Youthful Restraint**: Inject personality through typography, spacing, and subtle color tints, avoiding overly aggressive or chaotic elements.
3. **Context-Aware Adaptability**: Ensure high contrast and legibility across both light and dark themes to suit bright daytime and dark nighttime environments.
